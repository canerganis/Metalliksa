## 2026-09-27 — IN718 independent-measurement candidate audit
- Yang et al. 2025 Case C is the strongest newly found conduction-mode candidate: 195 W, 1200 mm/s, wrought bare plate, estimated D4σ 100 µm, 10 mm scans; authors estimate a 56 µm width/84 µm depth boundary from EBSD. Source repeats four tracks and thermography aggregates 42 pixel curves; data availability directs further inquiries to authors. Exact beam profile, raw series, boundary equivalence, and measurement uncertainty for W/D are insufficient to admit it. No model comparison was run; `unvalidated` remains. Official source https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=958155.
- NIST AM-Bench 2022 publishes bare-plate geometry with an uncertainty budget, but its 285 W / 960 mm/s / 67 µm baseline and aspect-ratio regime differ from this model's current case; each candidate still needs matched inputs and observable definition. Source https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=957295.
