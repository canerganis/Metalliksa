**Environment.** Every parity/bump step ran in the locked reference interpreter `.runtime/lpbf-win-py312` (CPython 3.12.10, NumPy 2.2.6, **no `warp` module**), i.e. the recorded golden environment. The skip guard in `tools/lpbf_parity_check.py` is unchanged (harness change not part of this bump; after this bump the guarded paths are host-independent, so the guard could be narrowed in a separate, reviewed harness change).
