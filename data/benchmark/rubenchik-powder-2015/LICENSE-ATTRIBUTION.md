Experiment: powder layer in a rimmed Ta disc, uniformly irradiated by 970 nm VCSELs (flat top over the 1 cm
aperture) at 8.7-13.8 W/cm^2, in air, up to about 500 C; calorimetric absorptivity from the heating and cooling
curves. No melting and no scanning: this is cold powder-layer absorptivity, not melt-pool coupling. The text gives
about 70 % for Ti-6Al-4V and agreement with the 58-60 % ray-tracing values for 316 SS.
